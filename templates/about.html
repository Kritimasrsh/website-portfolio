MY STORY

I like understanding what happens behind the interface.

My interest in development started with a simple question: how does a website actually work beyond what we see on the screen? That curiosity gradually led me from basic web development to Python, Django, databases and APIs.

Backend development became the part I enjoyed most. I like thinking about how data should be structured, how requests move through an application, how users interact with a system, and how different pieces of software communicate with each other.

I prefer learning through projects. Building something from scratch forces me to solve real problems, read documentation, debug mistakes and understand why a solution works instead of simply memorizing syntax.

“The best way for me to understand something is to build it.”
